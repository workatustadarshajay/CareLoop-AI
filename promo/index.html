01 — Start with the note

One conversation.
A plan that makes sense.

A clinician's note holds a lot: a test to book, a medicine to start, a person to call. CareLoop brings those instructions into one clear plan, written for the people who need to act on it.

to a clear next step.

02 — Keep the steps connected

Because the next
step depends on this one.

Tests, referrals and follow-ups do not happen in isolation. CareLoop keeps the relationships visible, so a delay in one step does not quietly stall the rest.
